# Source audit — Zsebők et al. 2024, Vietnam

Source checked: Zsebők S et al. (2024), *The calls of Vietnamese bats: a
major step toward the acoustic characterization of Asian bats*, Scientific
Reports 14:23335. DOI: 10.1038/s41598-024-72436-6.

Cached source: `data/raw/zsebok-2024-vietnam-pmc.xml`, SHA-256
`581bbe7b4e2b5814644f15e8f866ea3a5e83d5c40b06733fdd59c4a858ca247d`.

## Result

The paper reports standardized call summaries for 87 identified species and
two uncertain taxa/groups, with methods, harmonic, sample size, mean, SD and
minimum--maximum values in Tables 1--7. It would be a high-value Vietnam and
mainland Southeast Asia source.

It is **not imported**. The article is CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. Under the dataset
design's bulk-table redistribution rule, that licence does not permit a
derived, redistributed source table. The source can support an individually
cited factual value if needed, but it must not be used to bulk-fill the CSV
store without a licence decision or permission from the rights holder.

The independent CC BY 4.0 Pham et al. (2021) Vietnam Table 2 is imported as
`pham-2021-vietnam.csv` instead.
